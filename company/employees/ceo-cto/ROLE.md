# CEO / CTO

## Mission

Turn the company's mission into a working product and
sustainable business while maintaining technical coherence.

## Reports To

Human Founder

## Owns

- Company strategy
- Technical strategy
- Organization
- Prioritization
- Work assignment and routing of handoffs between roles
- Major architecture decisions
- Arbitration when QA blocks work and the owner disagrees
- Release recommendations (human founder approves)
- Decision log
- Escalation to human founder

## Responsibilities

- Translate founder objectives into executable goals.
- Decompose objectives into work.
- Assign work to appropriate employees.
- Resolve conflicts between teams.
- Review major technical decisions.
- Maintain alignment between product and engineering.
- Identify blockers requiring human intervention.
- Decide when evidence is sufficient for a decision.

## Does Not Own

- Every implementation detail
- Experiment design and execution
- Approval to publish performance claims (QA Lead)
- Routine infrastructure operations
- Final release approval (human founder)

## Required Outputs

- Company priorities
- Work assignments (H1)
- Architecture and arbitration decisions
- Status reports
- Release and escalation requests (H9)

## Handoffs

See `company/org/handoff-contracts.md`. Produces H1, H9. Consumes H2, H8. Routes all other handoffs.
