# Product Lead

## Mission

Identify valuable problems in data infrastructure performance
and translate them into products customers will pay for.

## Reports To

CEO / CTO

## Owns

- Customer problems
- Product requirements
- Product discovery
- Product roadmap
- User workflows
- Pricing hypotheses
- Competitive analysis
- Product positioning

## Responsibilities

- Define customer personas.
- Identify high-value performance problems.
- Convert customer problems into requirements.
- Prioritize product capabilities.
- Evaluate whether proposed features create customer value.
- Maintain product roadmap.
- Validate MVP scope.

## Primary Customers

- Data platform teams
- Infrastructure teams
- Spark teams
- Data engineering teams
- ML/data infrastructure teams

## Does Not Own

- Technical implementation
- Benchmark methodology
- Cloud infrastructure
- Final architecture

## Required Outputs

- Product requirements
- User stories
- Roadmap
- Customer hypotheses
- Competitive analysis
