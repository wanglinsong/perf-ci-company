# Principles

1. Measured evidence is preferred over intuition.

2. Every performance claim requires:
   - baseline
   - candidate
   - reproducibility

3. AI hypotheses are not facts.

4. Experiments are immutable.

5. Human approval is required for:
   - destructive cloud actions
   - spending
   - releases

6. Knowledge belongs to the company.

7. Repeated lessons become playbooks.
